## English (en-GB, also used for en-US)

**Title** (30 characters at most):

Biblelastic: Bible study

**Short description** (80 at most):

Free Bible study: the Open English Translation, commentaries and dictionaries

**Full description** (4,000 at most):

Biblelastic is a free Bible study app built around the Open English Translation (OET), a new, openly licensed translation of the Bible into modern English.

Read the OET's Readers' Version, its Literal Version, or both together, a section at a time. Tap a word to see the Greek or Hebrew word it translates. Tap a verse number to see what your commentaries, study notes and dictionaries say about that verse.

Search by words, or by meaning: meaning search finds passages about a subject even when they use other words, and it runs entirely on your phone.

WHAT YOU CAN DOWNLOAD
• Bibles: the Open English Translation, the World English Bible, the Berean Standard Bible, the King James Bible, Young's Literal Translation, the Geneva Bible, Tyndale's and Wycliffe's translations and more, as well as the Greek New Testament and the Hebrew Bible, and two Spanish Bibles.
• Commentaries and study notes: Tyndale Open Study Notes, Matthew Henry, Calvin, Keil and Delitzsch, Adam Clarke, Jamieson, Fausset and Brown, Barnes, Wesley, the Treasury of David, the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge and more.
• Dictionaries: the Tyndale Open Bible Dictionary, Easton's, Smith's, Nave's Topical Bible and more.
• Books: a library of classic Christian books in the public domain.

You choose what to download, and once it's on your phone it works without an internet connection. Every work shows its copyright, licence and source. Everything in the catalogue is free to read and share.

FREE AND PRIVATE
Biblelastic is free software from Freely-Given.org, under the GNU General Public License. It has no ads and no account, and it collects no personal information. Its source code is at github.com/Freely-Given-org/Biblelastic

## Answers for the Play Console's questions

- **Ads:** no ads.
- **App access:** everything can be used without signing in (there are no accounts).
- **Target audience:** 13 and over (13–15, 16–17, 18+). Leaving out under-13s keeps the app out of the Families programme's extra rules.
- **Content rating (IARC):** a reference app. The Bible and its commentaries describe violence and sexual matters as historical and religious text, not as entertainment; there's no user-to-user communication, no sharing of location, no purchases and no gambling.
- **Data safety:**
  - Does the app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No.** It sends nothing about the user: only requests for files from Freely-Given.org (see the privacy policy for what Cloudflare and the server's logs see).
  - Is data encrypted in transit? **Yes** (HTTPS).
  - Account deletion: not applicable (no accounts).
- **News app, government app, financial features, health features:** no.
- **Permissions:** internet only, which needs no declaration.
